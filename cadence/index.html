Questions

Before you book

Who is Cadence Club for?

Founders who have already done the work with me, usually through Sales Club. It's invitation only, because it builds on the system you made there.

How is it different from Sales Club?

Sales Club is the 12-week programme where you build your sales and marketing system. Cadence Club is the ongoing group that keeps it running, with your numbers, your commitments and a weekly rhythm.

When do sessions run?

The group meets for one hour on Zoom every other week. Between sessions, the Monday check-in and Friday wins happen in the Cadence WhatsApp group.

How long do I need to commit for?

It's a rolling monthly membership with a three-month minimum. Three months is long enough for the rhythm to stick and for your numbers to show it.

Is there a discount for paying upfront?

Yes. Pay for six months upfront and you get 10% off.

Can I add 1:1 time?

Yes. Founding members can add 1:1 time with me at £100 + VAT an hour, half my usual rate, held for as long as you stay.

What if I miss a session?

Life happens. The WhatsApp group keeps you connected, and you can still log your numbers every Monday wherever you are.

Do I need any special tech or tools?

Just Zoom and WhatsApp. That's it.
